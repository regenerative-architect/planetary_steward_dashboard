    Near You

    What the world's data means close to home: weather, sunlight, air quality, and any earthquakes, natural hazards and disaster alerts near a place you choose. Nothing is looked up until you choose a place.

    Your privacy here

    Choosing a place sends only that place's name (if you search for one) and its coordinates, rounded to about 1 km, to Open-Meteo — a free public weather service. For official alerts, the same rounded coordinates go only to the U.S. National Weather Service (places in the U.S.), Environment and Climate Change Canada (places in Canada) or Germany's weather service (places in Germany). For other countries this page downloads that country's whole official alert feed and checks your place itself, so your place isn't sent. Near the sea, the rounded coordinates also go to Open-Meteo's ocean model. Checking for nearby earthquakes, hazards and alerts happens entirely in your browser. Your place stays in memory only, unless you tick "Remember". You can clear it at any time.

    📋 Share a calm summary

    Today’s figures, each with its status and source — a calmer alternative to forwarding a scary headline. Nothing is sent until you choose where to paste or share it.

    ⏸️ Before you share — a short pause
    • Where does it come from? Each line names its source.
    • Is it true? Each line says whether it is live, a saved copy or reference — check the source if you’re unsure.
    • When was it written? This summary is dated; figures change, so yesterday’s copy may be out of date.
    • Why am I sharing it? To inform and connect, not to alarm — think of how it will feel to the person reading it.
